1. **Theorem 1 (`[PROVEN (computer-assisted)]`):** All $880$ complex planar realizations of the unique maximally-realizable $10$-vertex Laman graph $L_{880}$ ([OEIS A306420](https://oeis.org/A306420), encoding `4778440734593` in [Grasegger, Koutschan & Tsigaridas 2020, Table 8](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7324120/)) can be simultaneously real. Combined with our standalone implementation of Theorem 4.7 of [Capco et al. (2018)](https://arxiv.org/html/1701.05500v2) (`scripts/verify_capco_laman.py`) proving $c_2(L_{880}) = 880$, this settles $\varphi_{10} = 1$ on Open Problem 5 of [Grasegger, Koutschan & Tsigaridas (2020)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7324120/) and answers this repository's headline open question on $n = 10$.
2. **Corollary 2 (`[PROVEN (computer-assisted)]`):** By the Fan Construction Theorem (gluing $k = \lfloor (n-3)/7 \rfloor$ copies of $(L_{880}, \lambda^\star)$ along the shared rational triangle $\{2,3,7\}$ plus $m = (n-3)\bmod 7$ Henneberg-I steps; [Bartzos et al. 2018, Theorem 3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1811.12800); [Grasegger et al. 2020, Formula (2)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7324120/)), $r_2(n) \ge 2^{(n-3)\bmod 7} \cdot 2 \cdot 440^{\lfloor (n-3)/7 \rfloor}$ for every $n \ge 3$, raising the certified asymptotic lower bound on $\liminf r_2(n)^{1/n}$ from $430^{1/7} \approx 2.377989$ to $440^{1/7} \approx 2.385811$ (about $+0.33\%$ on the base).
