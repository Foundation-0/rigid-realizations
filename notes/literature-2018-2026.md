- Capco, Gallet, Grasegger, Koutschan, Lubbes & Schicho (2018, arXiv:1701.05500): Established the combinatorial algorithm determining the exact number of complex planar realizations c_2(G) of minimally rigid (Laman) graphs, proving the maximal counts c_2(6) = 24 (Prism_6), c_2(7) = 56, c_2(8) = 136 (L136), c_2(9) = 344 (L344), c_2(10) = 880 (L880), c_2(11) = 2,288 (L2288), and c_2(12) = 6,180 (OEIS A306420).
- Grasegger, Koutschan & Tsigaridas (Exp. Math. 2020; arXiv:1710.08237): Open Problem 5 defines φ_n = R_2(G_{n,max})/M_2(n), notes φ_n = 1 for n ≤ 7, and asks whether φ_n = 1 for all n.
- Bartzos, Emiris, Legersky & Tsigaridas (2018 / 2021, arXiv:1811.12800, Journal of Symbolic Computation): Established total reality up to n = 9 (r_2(8) = 136, r_2(9) = 344), obtained 860 Maple-certified real realizations (868 uncertified numerical solutions via phcpy) for L880 (n = 10), state the asymptotic planar lower bound 2^((n−3) mod 7)·2·430^⌊(n−3)/7⌋ (equivalently base 430^(1/7) ≈ 2.377989^n), and 132 real realizations for the maximal 8-vertex 3D graph G160 (ISSAC 2018, arXiv:1802.05860; Maple-verified per the authors).
- Grasegger (2025, arXiv:2502.04736): Improves the complex lower bounds (2.59972^n in the plane) and cites ≈2.378^n (plane) and 2.6553^n (space) for real counts from Bartzos et al.; it does not discuss L880's 860/868 or G160's 132.
