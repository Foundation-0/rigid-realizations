- Capco, Gallet, Grasegger, Koutschan, Lubbes & Schicho (2018, arXiv:1701.05500): Established the combinatorial algorithm determining the exact number of complex planar realizations c_2(G) of minimally rigid (Laman) graphs, proving the maximal counts c_2(6) = 24 (Prism_6), c_2(7) = 56, c_2(8) = 136 (L136), c_2(9) = 344 (L344), c_2(10) = 880 (L880), c_2(11) = 2,288 (L2288), and c_2(12) = 6,180 (OEIS A306420).
- Grasegger, Koutschan & Tsigaridas (2020, arXiv:1803.06982): Proved lower bounds on complex realization counts and posed Open Problem 5 asking whether r_2(n) = c_2(n) (equivalently phi_n = 1) continues to hold at n = 10 and beyond.
- Bartzos, Emiris, Legersky & Tsigaridas (2018 / 2021, arXiv:1811.12800, Journal of Symbolic Computation): Established total reality up to n = 9 (r_2(8) = 136, r_2(9) = 344), obtained 860 Maple-certified real realizations (868 uncertified numerical solutions via phcpy) for L880 (n = 10), r_2(13) >= 10,320 by gluing Prism_6 onto their 860-real L880, the asymptotic planar lower bound 430^(1/7) ≈ 2.377989^n, and 132 numerical real realizations out of 160 complex realizations for the maximal 8-vertex 3D graph G160.
- Grasegger (2025, arXiv:2502.04736): Surveyed planar and spatial realization counts through 2025. Table 1 records the Bartzos baseline as r_2(L880) ≥ 860 (Maple-certified) with 868 from homotopy continuation listed separately, not as a proven count; for G160 it reports 132 numerical realizations out of 160 complex—figures the certifications in this repository tighten but do not themselves prove exact totals.
