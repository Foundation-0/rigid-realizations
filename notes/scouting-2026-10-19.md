# Scouting record, 19 October 2026

The research behind this project, from Foundation0's
[scouting of 18–19 October 2026](https://github.com/Foundation-0/f0/tree/main/deep-fields). Each fact is marked by
how far it has been checked.

## The question

Can bar lengths be chosen so that all 880 complex realizations of the 10-vertex rigid framework are real? How many
real realizations can minimally rigid graphs reach in the plane and in space?

A framework is a set of points joined by bars of fixed length. A minimally rigid (Laman) graph with given bar
lengths can be built in finitely many ways, up to moving it as a whole. Counting with complex numbers gives an upper
bound. Only the real solutions are physical shapes. For 8 and 9 vertices, lengths are known for which every solution
is real. For 10 vertices, 860 of the 880 have been shown real.

Hubs: [physics0](https://github.com/Foundation-0/physics0) and [math0](https://github.com/Foundation-0/math0).
Related projects: [incentre-lattices](https://github.com/Foundation-0/incentre-lattices) (rigidity) and
[point-charges](https://github.com/Foundation-0/point-charges) (certified root counting).

## Checked on 19 Oct 2026

- Bartzos, Emiris, Legerský and Tsigaridas, [arXiv:1811.12800](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.12800), table in §4.1.
  Here c2 is the number of complex realizations in the plane and r2 the number of real ones.

  | Vertices | Graph | c2 | r2 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 8 | L136 | 136 | ≥ 136 |
  | 9 | L344 | 344 | ≥ 344 |
  | 10 | L880 | 880 | ≥ 860 |

- The paper states: "there exist edge lengths such that all embeddings of the 8-vertex graph L136 and of the 9-vertex
  graph L344 are real."
- On L880: "phcpy gives 868 real solutions ... we were able to verify only 860 ... we take that r2(L880) ≥ 860."

## From the scouting report, not yet re-checked

- Grasegger ([arXiv:2502.04736](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.04736), Feb 2025) settled the complex maxima for all
  n ≤ 13. He wrote that the complex-counting methods give little information about real counts. The real growth rate
  cited is about 2.378^n, against at least 2.5997^n for complex counts.
- In space, the 8-vertex graph G160 has 160 complex embeddings, and 132 real ones have been found.
- Some graphs provably have fewer real than complex realizations. The known obstruction (4 does not divide c2) does
  not apply to 880.
- Code and tools:
  - Bartzos and Legerský's code on Zenodo (reference [2] of arXiv:1811.12800);
  - graph encodings in the appendix of Grasegger 2025;
  - [HomotopyContinuation.jl](https://www.juliahomotopycontinuation.org/) and its `certify` function;
  - PHCpack.
- Suggested stopping rule: no gain beyond 860 after two weeks and no visible obstruction.
